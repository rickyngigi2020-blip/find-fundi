The problem

Kenya's repair trade runs on word of mouth.

Someone's cousin knows a guy. He might show up. He might not be any good. There's no way to check his work, his price, or whether he'll come back if the fix doesn't hold.

Without Find Fundi

  • No way to check their ID before they're in your house
  • Price is whatever they decide to quote on the day
  • No record if the job goes wrong, no one to call

With Find Fundi

  • Every fundi's ID and documents are checked before they're approved
  • You see the price in the app and accept it before work starts
  • Each job is recorded, with the fundi's name, number and agreed price

How it works

Three steps, start to fixed.

01

Describe the problem

Type it or record a voice note, add photos, and drop a pin on your location.

02

Pick your fundi

Choose a verified fundi who does that kind of work. They call you to understand the job.

03

Agree the price, get it fixed

Accept the fundi's price in the app, pay them directly, and rate the work when it's done.
